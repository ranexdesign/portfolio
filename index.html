My design process

  1. ResearchTalk to users, study the problem, and audit what already exists.
  2. DefineTurn findings into personas, pain points, and a clear problem statement.
  3. StructureMap user flows and information architecture, then sketch wireframes.
  4. DesignBuild high-fidelity UI with consistent type, spacing, and components.
  5. Test & refinePrototype, gather feedback, and improve until the flow feels effortless.
